Know exactly what top players of your spec do, right on your character panel. Recommended Stats compares your Haste, Critical Strike, Mastery and Versatility with real top players of your class and spec, and shows the BiS gear, enchants, gems, talents and rotation they actually use, per raid boss and per Mythic+ dungeon. Then compare your own raid pulls with theirs.

  • Stat targets: see straight away which secondary stat is too low, on target or over.
  • BiS gear: the top item for every slot, with the enchant and gem top players use.
  • Talents: the build top players run on each boss and dungeon, copied in one click.
  • Rotation guide: the opener, core rotation, filler and cooldown timing, read from real Mythic kills.
  • Your pulls vs the top players: what to work on first, from your own Warcraft Logs.

Open your character panel (C) and it's there. No setup, no weights to paste.

New: Compare Your Own Pulls
Click My pulls on the Rotation window (or type /rs link) for the link to your page on the RecommendedStats Analyzer. It lists your raid nights from Warcraft Logs with every kill and wipe. Pick a pull to see the biggest differences from the top players of your spec on that boss first, then your casts, cooldowns and buffs side by side with theirs. Your raid needs to be logged on Warcraft Logs, by you or anyone in it.

Stat Targets for Raid and Mythic+
Haste, Critical Strike, Mastery and Versatility are compared live against target percentages built from top players of your class and spec. Colour-coded bars with a target tick show how far off you are, not just whether you're under or over. Switch between Raid and Mythic+ from the dropdown, or with /rs raid and /rs mythicplus. A stat priority line gives the recommended order from the same players.

Best-in-Slot Gear, Enchants and Gems
The BiS window shows the top item for every slot with how many of the top players wear it, the enchant and gem they use, and a check for whether your gear already has them. Matching looks at item level as well as the exact item, so a comparable upgrade is never wrongly flagged as missing. Tier set tracking shows how many pieces you wear and how many top players run the 4-piece.

Talents, Per Boss and Per Dungeon
The Talents window shows the build top players run, laid out like the in-game talent page: class, hero and spec trees. Pick a raid boss or Mythic+ dungeon, or stay on Overall for the spec-wide build. Hover any talent to see how many players take it, and copy the loadout string to import it in one click. When a boss or dungeon doesn't have enough exact data yet, it says so and shows the closest real build instead.

Rotation Guide, Per Boss
The Rotation window shows how top players of your spec and hero talents play it, read from what they pressed on real raid kills: the opener, the core of the rotation, the filler and when to use cooldowns. It shows which press gives each proc or buff, which press spends it, and how many stacks to build first. Every spell is an icon with its real in-game tooltip.

The rotation guide won't increase your DPS by itself. It is a reference to grow your knowledge of the class and what takes priority, not a "press this now" helper, and it reads nothing about the fight you are in.

Make It Yours
Four row sizes, from one compact line to a full readout. A Default, Class Color or Custom Color skin, which you can export as a short code for friends. Colorblind-friendly icons for the stat and BiS marks. Windows dock to the character panel or move anywhere, and remember their position per character. A minimap button (LibDataBroker) opens everything with one click.

Where the Numbers Come From
Every target, BiS pick, talent build and rotation comes from real top-player data, not guesswork. Warcraft Logs surfaces the top parsers on every raid boss and the top Mythic+ players in every dungeon, Raider.IO supplies the talent builds from real kills and runs, and Blizzard's API supplies each player's exact stats, gear and talents. The rotation guide is read from the Warcraft Logs combat logs of top-parsing raid kills. The addon never contacts a server: the data ships inside it and refreshes with each update. The panel always shows how many players a target is built from.

Commands

  • /rs raid or /rs mythicplus: switch content
  • /rs bis, /rs talents, /rs rotation: open each window
  • /rs link: your page on the RecommendedStats Analyzer
  • /rs resetpos: snap windows back to their default spot
  • /rs options: options (also the minimap button's right-click)
  • /rs skin export and /rs skin import: share a skin

Good to Know

  • Raid and Mythic+ targets are rebuilt regularly as the season goes on. Early in a tier, or for a rarely played spec, a target may rest on fewer players, and the panel says how many.
  • The rotation guide covers raid bosses and follows your hero talents. If too few top players run yours, it shows the tree they do run and says so.
  • Healers see the rotation and filler only, since healing cooldowns follow the boss's damage rather than a fixed timing.
  • The rotation guide is refreshed about once a month or after a patch, and shows the patch and date it was read on.
  • Data sourced in part from Warcraft Logs and Raider.IO.
